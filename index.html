TL;DR - Who am I ? An Engineer, Artist & Thinker.

Hi, I'm Lucie, a curious mind driven by abstraction. I'm fascinated by patterns that connect seemingly unrelated fields. My long-term goal is to understand deeply at all scales, enjoy arts and explore nature.

I recently graduated with an MEng in Computer Science from Telecom Nancy (French Grande École d'Ingénieur) and completed an ML Engineer internship at Samsung Electronics. I'm currently in Italy, pursuing the International MSc in Physics of Complex Systems from Sorbonne, Politecnico di Torino & SISSA.

Over the last 5 years, I learned about the fundamentals of Maths, Physics, CS & Cybersecurity. For the coming years, I'm focusing on Frontier AI, Statistical Physics & Active Matter. Recommendations upon request at contact.
